WallCtrl

Private wall panel app for a single household

WallCtrl is a private Android app that runs on one wall-mounted phone in the owner's home. It shows a few large buttons to switch the owner's own lights and blinds that are already connected to Google Home, and to run two daily routines (evening and morning).

The app uses the Google Home APIs only to read the state of these devices and to send on/off and blind-position commands to them. It is not published in any app store and is not offered to the public.
