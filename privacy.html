WallCtrl Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

WallCtrl is a private app used by one household on one wall-mounted device. It is not distributed to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the user's permission, WallCtrl uses the Google Home APIs to read the names and current state of the household's own smart home devices (lights and blinds) and to send commands to them (on/off, blind position, and the household's own routines).

How the data is used and stored

This data is used only on the device to show the current state and to control the devices. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or sent to any server other than Google's own services. The app keeps a technical log on the device for troubleshooting.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings (Data & privacy, Third-party apps & services) or in the Google Home app.
